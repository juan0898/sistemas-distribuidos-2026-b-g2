## 2. My individual contribution

* Designed and documented the relational data model for the Billing Service.
* Defined `bill` as the main entity responsible for representing invoices associated with patients.
* Defined the relationship between `bill` and `invoice_detail` to support multiple procedures within the same invoice.
* Modeled the `procedure` entity to store the procedures available for billing.
* Defined `pay` and `payment_method` entities to support payment registration and identify the payment method used.
* Defined `invoice_status` to control the invoice lifecycle, including states such as pending, paid, and cancelled.
* Established primary keys using UUIDs and foreign-key relationships between the entities.
* Documented the responsibilities and relationships of the billing entities.
* Created the billing database diagram to visually represent the entities and their relationships.

## 3. Blockers and risks

* The billing model must remain consistent with the patient and clinical services because invoices depend on information generated by those domains.
* Integration contracts between the Billing Service and other services still need to be validated.
* Business rules for invoice status transitions and payment validation must be clearly defined before implementation.
* Changes to procedures or patient identifiers in other services could require adjustments to the billing model.
